## Summary

The deck opens with a title slide and then a page of investment disclaimers and representations that an investor must accept, followed by the company's one-line mission of turning carbon dioxide into protein with microbes. It then moves through the problem of rising protein demand and pressure on arable land, the proposed solution of single cell protein (Proton) made from gases, and why the timing is now, linked to the energy transition. Market sizing follows, covering total addressable and serviceable markets, a comparison with peer fermentation approaches, and traction from pilot-scale production and partnerships. Later sections describe the business model (licensing, joint ventures and owned plants), the team and locations, a scale-up roadmap from lab to commercial plants with the Series A funding for a demonstration facility, and a closing list of reasons to invest. The deck ends with contact details. Several slides are dense with tables and bulleted claims, and the market and roadmap slides are the most useful to study for layout and information design.

**How does Aerbio's deck describe its business model?**

Slide 12 describes a short-term own and operate model (preferred), a mid-term joint venture model if necessary, and a long-term licensing opportunity where Aerbio licenses its technology and receives royalties. The slide says the company plans to pursue an asset light business model via licensing, but that at least one fully owned and operated commercial plant will be required to prove the concept at scale.
