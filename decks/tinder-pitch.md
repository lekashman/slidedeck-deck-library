## Summary

The deck opens with a title slide for Match Box, a flirting game, then tells the story of Matt, who sees someone he likes at a party but does not approach her because he fears rejection. It presents the product as the solution: users like people nearby and get connected when the feeling is mutual, with sign-in through Facebook. The middle slides walk through the core experience in sequence: a Like or Not card screen showing shared interests and friends, a matches list showing who likes you back, and a chat screen where two matched users begin talking. The next slide covers the matching signals, including hyper-location using WiFi rather than GPS, mutual liking, shared interests, and friends in common. The deck closes with a revenue slide listing in-app purchases such as two free matches, a fee to reveal each further match, paid virtual gifts, and paid placement higher in the Like or Not list. Slides 6 to 8 are the strongest for study, as they show the product flow with real screens and a short example conversation.
