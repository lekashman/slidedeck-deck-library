## Review

**7 out of 10, Effective.** Presentation quality, judged with the SlideDeck Co review heuristic (https://slidedeck.co/review-methodology/). The ask, the evidence and the merit of the idea are not rated.

The deck has a clear, well-ordered pitch route, and each slide carries one idea in large type with a consistent blue and pink identity. Slides 3, 5, 7 and 14 turn the story into simple visual forms (three tiles, sized circles, a chain from $84M to $25 to $2.1B, and $500K to 80K to $2M) that are easy to follow. Weaknesses are real, though: the repeated tiny contact footer and the card frame add little, many slides leave large empty areas (1, 4, 12), and slide 4 gives two unrelated figures with no comparison or stated meaning. Slide 9's positioning map is crowded with small logos and unreadable labels, and slide 8 has tiny screenshots and uneven balance, so some pages need explanation from a speaker.

**Worth keeping:** Keep the large pink and blue headings, the single-idea slides, the circle-and-arrow number sequences on slides 5, 7 and 14, and the three-step product flow on slide 6.

**Most useful repair:** Enlarge or simplify the slide 9 map and the slide 8 screenshots, and add a takeaway line to slide 4 so the two numbers read as one point.

**What would change the score:** I would drop the contact footer from every slide but the first and last, and use the empty space on slides 1, 4 and 12 for larger type or imagery.
