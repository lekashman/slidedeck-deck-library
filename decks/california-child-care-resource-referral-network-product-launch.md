## Summary

The deck opens with a problem statement: online child care searches are confusing, hard to compare, and may not be reliable. It then introduces MyChildCarePlan.org as a state-funded, no-cost consumer education tool available in four languages. The middle sections explain how the tool works, including search filters, saved searches and personalized plans, and how families can get one-on-one help from local Child Care Resource & Referral Agencies. Next it describes what those agencies are and the free and paid tiers of referral services, including a search widget that employers can add to their own websites. A preview section then walks through the main features with screen-style slides covering search, resource libraries, and parent account tools. The deck closes with a summary of benefits and a contact slide for the digital project manager. Slides worth studying include the problem-and-solution opening on slides 2 and 3, the step-by-step explanation on slide 5, the tier comparison on slides 8 through 10, and the feature preview on slides 12 through 14, which shows the product through simple labeled visuals rather than dense text.
