## Review

**6 out of 10, Needs substantial work.** Presentation quality, judged with the SlideDeck Co review heuristic (https://slidedeck.co/review-methodology/). The ask, the evidence and the merit of the idea are not rated.

The deck has a consistent navy, red and white identity, a clear route from company intro to guarantee, ACH, RDC, security, integrations and onboarding, and several well-composed pages (title on slide 1, onboarding steps on slide 8). But many slides are thin or too small to read for a presented deck: the About Us body text on slide 2 and the table text on slide 5 are tiny, and the security paragraph on slide 6 is a small block of prose. Slide 3 is mostly empty, slide 4 is a donut that implies proportion where there is none, and the slide 2 checkmarks look like progress states with only the first one highlighted, so the deck reads more like a leave-behind brochure than a persuasive sales talk.

**Worth keeping:** Keep the navy and red identity, the full-bleed title and closing slides, and the numbered three-step onboarding layout with photo on slide 8.

**Most useful repair:** Enlarge the body text on slides 2, 5 and 6, turn the slide 6 paragraph into a few headed points, and make the slide 2 checkmarks equal in state.

**What would change the score:** Replace the donut on slide 4 with three equal option cards, and give slide 3 a simple visual of what the guarantee covers so the central selling point carries weight.
