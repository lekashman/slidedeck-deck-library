## Review

**6 out of 10, Needs substantial work.** Presentation quality, judged with the SlideDeck Co review heuristic (https://slidedeck.co/review-methodology/). The ask, the evidence and the merit of the idea are not rated.

The deck has a clear story from problem to mechanism to product, competitors, market, plan, team and ask, and several slides are well built: the competitor table on slide 7 is easy to read and the pitch is rendered consistently in blue with a repeated footer. But slide 2 puts the problem and solution in small dense text beside four crowded photos, and slide 3 mixes a pathway diagram with a cartoon whose purpose is unclear. The timeline on slide 12 and the pie chart on slide 16 are crowded, with tiny labels and overlapping markers, and slides 10, 11 and 13 rely on small text that would be hard to read in a room. The core case is recoverable, but a presenter would need to tidy many slides and fix the hierarchy before the deck is easy to follow.

**Worth keeping:** Keep the green and red comparison table on slide 7, the headline-as-claim titles in blue, the consistent footer and logo, and the sequence from problem through mechanism to ask.

**Most useful repair:** Simplify slide 2 into a single headline with one image, enlarge and declutter the timeline on slide 12, and replace the pie on slide 16 with a sorted bar chart or a grouped list with larger labels.

**What would change the score:** I would remove the cartoon on slide 3, split slide 11 into fewer groups with bigger text, and drop the small caption text on slide 5 or move it to a footnote.

## Summary

The deck opens with the company name and a title slide, then states the problem: impaired sensory processing from aging, neurological disorders, fatigue and injury reduces acuity and raises fall risk. It presents a noninvasive vagus nerve stimulation patch as the solution. Next it explains the mechanism through a brain-pathway diagram, lists the drawbacks of alternatives such as stimulants, nootropics and therapy, and cites the academic papers behind the approach. The product section covers form factor, features and target price, followed by a competitor comparison table of VNS devices by application, form factor and stimulation location. The deck then describes the consumer impact of age-related sensory decline and sizes adjacent markets. A revenue and gross margin projection table from 2024 to 2029 is followed by a regulatory and reimbursement plan, expansion markets, a timeline of research, funding and milestones, a proposed pilot study, the founding team and partner institutions. It closes with a seed round ask of $400K and contact details.
