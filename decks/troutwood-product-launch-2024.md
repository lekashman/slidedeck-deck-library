## Summary

The deck opens with a title slide, "A Financial Plan For Life," and a definition of a financial plan as a way to set goals, follow a path and adjust as events occur. It then makes a case for financial literacy through three trends: micro investing, financial literacy classes in schools and financial wellness in the workplace. Next it covers long-term investing with a compounding return chart and three investment lessons for educators. A problem section cites statistics on teen employment, paycheck-to-paycheck living and low financial literacy, followed by a solution section presenting the Troutwood app and its three-step plan builder (profile, career, goal). The app section includes a green-shield likelihood and adequacy indicator and download instructions. The deck closes with educator support tools, a financial plan message, and a stock market simulation game called Buy, Sell, Hold! plus an interactive stock market map. Slides 4, 13 and 12 are the most worth studying for their visual design.
