## Review

**7 out of 10, Effective.** Presentation quality, judged with the SlideDeck Co review heuristic (https://slidedeck.co/review-methodology/). The ask, the evidence and the merit of the idea are not rated.

The deck has a strong, consistent identity (red shard motif, bold grey headings, game art) and a clear route from who we are, to what changed, to the pipeline, to the takeaways. Slide 23 is the standout summary, with one band per franchise and the owner of each title, and slides 1 and 28 are legible and well composed. Weaknesses are consequential: many slides are sparse (15, 19, 20, 22) or carry very small gray text (9, 10, 11, 17, 18, 21), and the pipeline slides repeat one template with little to compare, with no dates or timeline on slide 23 despite the title's 'outlook'. **Most useful repair:** Enlarge the small text on slides 9, 10, 17, 18 and 21, and add timing or stage cues to slide 23 so the outlook reads as a sequence.

**What would change the score:** I would merge the near-empty project slides (19, 20, 22) into a stage-by-stage pipeline view and redraw the flywheel on slide 13 as a clearer diagram.
