## Review

## Summary

The deck opens with a title slide and a short greeting, then uses a road-trip metaphor: product vision is the steering wheel, and engineering culture is the engine. It defines culture as the shared values of an organization shown through members' actions, and cites Henrik Kniberg's view that culture is what people do without noticing. A diagram links values to culture and then to processes, artifacts, rituals and beliefs. The middle section walks through Spotify's own values, shown as four illustrated tiles with the value words listed beside them, and quotes Tim O'Reilly and Cory Ondrejka on why culture needs deliberate attention as a company grows. Later slides show how Spotify's agile processes and organizational model follow from its values, and stress regular self-surveys and fixing problems early. The deck closes with the line that culture leads and everything else follows. Slides worth studying include the road-trip image on slide 3, the values diagram on slide 9, and the Tim O'Reilly quote slide on slide 12, which pairs a portrait with a single large statement.
