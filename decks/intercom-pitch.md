## Summary

The deck opens with a DRAFT title slide, then introduces the founding team and their track record in software design, writing and prior product launches. It moves to the problem: building meaningful relationships with customers is hard for SaaS providers, and existing tools for research, messaging and email fall short. The solution slide describes Intercom as a simple install that offers customer browsing, individual customer research, in-app messaging and relationship tracking. Market sizing follows, then a landscape slide groups competitors by category, such as social media management, customer feedback, email campaigns and user analytics. The progress slide reports a product in development and testing, a public beta plan and early beta feedback. The closing ask seeks a $600k convertible note, the 18 months of runway it buys and the plan for a further round. The final slide is an advertisement for a third-party funding deck collection and the agency that made the deck.
