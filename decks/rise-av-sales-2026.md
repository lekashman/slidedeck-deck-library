## Summary

The deck opens with a title slide reading "Closing the Skills Gap. Empowering Women in AV" and sponsorship opportunities for 2026. It then sets out the problem: the AV sector is ageing and underrepresents women, with women making up 9% of the workforce and 2% of technical roles, against a projected 2.3 million skilled worker shortfall. Next it presents Rise AV's goals and its five pillars of career paths, training, community, representation and corporate work. The middle sections describe the Mentoring and Elevate programmes, with outcome statistics from mentee follow-ups, and make the case that investing in women leaders benefits employers. A slide on what is coming in 2026 comes next, followed by the benefits of sponsorship, the sponsorship tiers from regional to global headline partner, and a table of what each tier includes. The deck closes with ways to support the organisation and a contact page. Slides 6 and 13 are the most worth studying: the first pairs each outcome figure with a single label, and the second uses a compact tier table to compare packages side by side.

THE CHALLENGE
The AV industry is an ageing, historically male-dominated sector where women
remain significantly underrepresented. Those who enter are often not adequately
supported and, in many cases, do not progress at the same rate as their male
counterparts. This reinforces perceptions that the sector lacks inclusivity and is not
attractive or accessible to women. While many organisations recognise the
importance of improving diversity and inclusion, they often lack the time, budget, and
internal capability to deliver meaningful, sustained change.
THE BARRIERS
Non-inclusive workplace cultures and ongoing unconscious bias experienced by
women. Limited investment in development beyond senior levels further restricts
progression, contributing to a “leaky pipeline” where women leave the industry faster
than they advance.
THE OPPORTUNITY
To fix both the talent pipeline and the system that is failing to retain it. The most
immediate and impactful way to address the skills gap is by attracting and retaining
more women, effectively doubling the available talent pool. The business case is clear:
diverse teams are 25% more profitable, make decisions 78% faster, and generate 19%
more revenue through innovation.
THE SOLUTION
Rise AV addresses this through two connected pillars: supporting women throughout
their careers and working directly with AV businesses to improve inclusion from the
inside. This includes upskilling the AV workforce, creating peer support networks,
breaking down barriers, and developing clear pathways for women to enter, stay, and
progress in the industry. With sponsor support, we can transform the AV workforce,
close the skills gap, and strengthen the industry for the future.
