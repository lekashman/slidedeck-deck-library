## Review

**5 out of 10, Needs substantial work.** Presentation quality, judged with the SlideDeck Co review heuristic (https://slidedeck.co/review-methodology/). The ask, the evidence and the merit of the idea are not rated.

The core story is recoverable: slide 2 sets out the problem from two sides, slide 3 offers three solution blocks, and slide 12 is a legible competitor table. But the middle of the deck fails as communication. Slides 5 to 10 are near-full-page product screenshots with tiny text, mostly in Indonesian, and the slide 5 title says 'Four Step' while the four steps are not shown as a sequence. Slide 2's number markers sit between unrelated left and right statements, and the slide 3 body text and the slide 11 market chain are small and loosely linked. The deck also lacks any traction or ask page and ends on bare logos and a small contact block.

**Worth keeping:** Keep the two-column truck owner versus cargo owner framing on slide 2, the three-part solution with icons on slide 3, and the full-width competitor table with logos on slide 12.

**Most useful repair:** Replace the screenshot slides 5 to 10 with a single four-step flow with large labels and cropped, annotated screens, and fix the slide 2 pairing so each numbered row reads as one problem.

**What would change the score:** Rework slide 11 into a clear market funnel with larger numbers and unit labels, and fix the title slide where the blue tagline over the truck and the dark photo hurts contrast.

## Summary

The deck opens with a title slide presenting Letz!Go as a platform connecting truck owners with B2B cargo owners. It then sets out the problem from both sides: cargo owners struggle to get working capital and depend on brokers, while truck owners find it hard to know truck availability and cannot pay a down payment. Next comes the solution, which has three parts: Letz!Go for matching trucks and cargo on one web platform, Letz!Track for a free transport management system, and Letz!Fund for down payments and payment terms. A market size slide and a four-step process for sending cargo follow, with slides 5 to 10 walking through order confirmation, driver notification, tracking and analytics dashboards. The business model, competitor table, competitive advantages and team slides come next. The deck closes with client and partner logos and contact details. Slides worth studying include the problem slide, the four-step process sequence, and the competitor comparison table.
