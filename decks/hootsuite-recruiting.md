## Review

**8 out of 10, Strong.** Presentation quality, judged with the SlideDeck Co review heuristic (https://slidedeck.co/review-methodology/). The ask, the evidence and the merit of the idea are not rated.

The deck has a clear route for a new-hire audience: principles (4-8), story (9-13), culture and #BSU (14-26), reasons people enjoy working there (27-40), then the three-chapter Peepsbook (41-63) and a closing call (64). Slides carry one idea each in large type, with section footers on the early pages and colour coding by chapter that keeps the reader oriented across 64 pages. Visuals are used with purpose: the flight path chart on slide 11 turns the growth story into one image, the bike illustration on 12 carries the metaphor, and the real staff portraits with name and role make the quotes feel credible. Weaknesses are a few thin or flat pages, such as the sparse list slides 7 and 8, the black numbered bars on 29, 31, 33, 35, 37 and 39 that look empty, and small light attribution text on 43 to 63, which keep it from the top tier.

**Worth keeping:** Keep the one-idea-per-slide rhythm, the large Vision and Mission statements on 4 and 5, the flight path chart on 11 and the colour-coded chapter system.

**Most useful repair:** Fill or remove the empty black bars above the numbered headings on 29 to 39, and enlarge the tiny attribution text and the faint section labels on 43 to 63.

**What would change the score:** I would give the value and team lists on 7 and 8 a visual treatment, and add axis scale or source detail to the growth chart on 11 so the 2011 to 2013 jump reads precisely.

## Summary

The deck is given to every new Hootsuite employee as a guide to the company's principles, history and culture. It opens with a title slide and short introduction, then moves through Core Principles covering vision, mission, culture, team and values. A Story section traces the founding from Invoke and the growth from 20 to over 600 employees. The Culture section explains the #BSU term and why people enjoy working there. The central Peepsbook is organised into three chapters, Who We Are, What We Do and How We Do It, with employee quotes, a list of suited traits, and short points on collaboration, trust and openness to failure. The deck closes with quotes from staff and leaders, including the founder, on curiosity and optimism. Its slides use large headline-style titles, one idea per slide, a consistent footer and simple charts. The Vision and Mission pages, with single large statements, and the flight path chart, which turns the growth story into one image, are worth studying.
