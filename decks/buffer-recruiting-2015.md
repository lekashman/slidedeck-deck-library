## Review

**5 out of 10, Needs substantial work.** Presentation quality, judged with the SlideDeck Co review heuristic (https://slidedeck.co/review-methodology/). The ask, the evidence and the merit of the idea are not rated.

The route is clear and orderly: title, why culture matters, origin, an index of ten values on slide 5, then one slide per value in the same order. Every page is black Gill Sans-style text on white with no image, color, diagram or emphasis, so nothing is visually memorable and the deck looks like a default template. Value slides 6 to 15 are identical bulleted lists, with small body text and a large empty lower third on slides 13 to 15, so the rhythm is monotonous and the key phrase in each bullet is not emphasised. It works as a readable reference document, but it takes substantial design work to communicate and be remembered as a presentation.

**Worth keeping:** Keep the index on slide 5 and the one-value-per-slide structure with the same title wording as the index, and the plain second-person bullets.

**Most useful repair:** Add a consistent identity (color, value numbers, a recurring marker linking each slide to slide 5), enlarge the body text, and bold the key phrase in each bullet.

**What would change the score:** I would give each value a stronger single statement, move the Dale Carnegie origin text on slide 4 to a lighter treatment, and fix the version mismatch (slides show 0.5 while the summary says 0.6) and the empty lower space on slides 13 to 15.

## Summary

The deck is a company culture document built as slides, titled The Buffer Culture and subtitled Powered by Happiness, version 0.6. It opens with a title slide and a note that the document is a continual work in progress, updated February 18, 2015. It then asks why culture matters, tells the origin of the values, and lists the ten Buffer values. The main body gives one slide per value, each with a short bulleted list of behaviours, covering positivity, transparency, self improvement, the no ego doer, listening, clarity, reflection, working smarter, gratitude and doing the right thing. Each value slide uses a headline style title and between four and five short bullets. The deck closes on the value of doing the right thing for customers and the company in the long term. Slides worth seeing include the value list on slide 5, which works as an index, and the listening slide 10, which shows how a bullet can be rewritten as a contrast of phrases.
