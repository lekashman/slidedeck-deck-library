## Review

**5 out of 10, Needs substantial work.** Presentation quality, judged with the SlideDeck Co review heuristic (https://slidedeck.co/review-methodology/). The ask, the evidence and the merit of the idea are not rated.

## Summary

The deck opens on a title slide with the tagline 'Broadcast Yourself' and a purpose statement: to become the primary outlet for user-generated video, letting anyone upload, share and browse it. It then names the problem: video files are too large to e-mail or host, lack a common format, and exist as isolated files. The solution slide describes uploading to a service that serves content to viewers, converts videos to Flash Video, and links users, videos and communities together. The market slide cites cheaper digital video recording and home broadband reaching critical mass. A competition slide names existing video sites. Product development and sales and distribution slides list planned features and revenue routes, including advertising, premium member features and paid content. The team slide profiles the three founders and their backgrounds. The deck closes with a metrics slide noting a June 11th launch and a claim of leading the space. Its slides are short and built around headline-style statements, and the market and solution slides are the most useful to study for structure.
