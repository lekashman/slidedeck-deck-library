## Review

**8 out of 10, Strong.** Presentation quality, judged with the SlideDeck Co review heuristic (https://slidedeck.co/review-methodology/). The ask, the evidence and the merit of the idea are not rated.

The deck carries a clear argument in order: values (4), three kinds of transformation (6 to 21), operating principles (26 to 33), employee voices (37 to 42), then growth and an invitation (43 to 48). A recognisable system holds it together: full-bleed photography, one light geometric typeface, colour-coded sections (blue self, purple company, red world, orange principles) and a repeated portrait-with-sign device. Text is short and set on tinted panels or colour washes, so it reads at a glance (9, 44), and the 2005 and 2014 photo pair (44, 46) makes the growth point with no chart. Weaknesses remain: small captions sit over busy photos (slides 11, 18, 33), type crosses faces and shirts on 7, 8 and 12, the closing run on 49 ends on a near-empty logo page, and slides 5, 36 and 40 rely on photos that do not obviously support their point.

**Worth keeping:** Keep the colour-coded sections, the full-bleed photography with short headline titles, the signs-in-hand portrait device, and the 2005 versus 2014 milestone pair.

**Most useful repair:** Enlarge or reposition the small captions on 11, 18 and 33 and move title text off faces and shirts on 7, 8 and 12.

**What would change the score:** I would drop or rework the blank logo page 49 and the lightly connected map on 35, and add a clear stated close tied to the recruiting action on 47 and 48.

## Summary

The deck opens with a full-bleed photo of employees and the title 'Our Culture', then frames culture as who the company is and aspires to be, with a short introduction to five values: Transformation, Integrity, Collaboration, Humor and Results. It is organised as three kinds of transformation: of self (career growth, speaker series, wellness), of company (women in tech, employee resource groups, all hands) and of world (the economic graph, LinkedIn for Good, InDay). Each value then gets its own slide with a short definition, followed by the six operating principles and a run of employee portrait slides with one-line quotes. The closing section ties culture to growth, citing member milestones from 2 million in 2005 to 300 million in 2014, and ends with a call to join and a hashtag. Slides rely on full-bleed photography, short headline-style titles and brief captions, so the story is carried visually as much as by text. Slides 5 to 7 and 12 to 13 are useful to study for how section dividers and portrait slides are paced.
