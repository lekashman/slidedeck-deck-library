## Review

**6 out of 10, Needs substantial work.** Presentation quality, judged with the SlideDeck Co review heuristic (https://slidedeck.co/review-methodology/). The ask, the evidence and the merit of the idea are not rated.

The deck has a clear route (intro, features, Interact, Service tag, drawings, specs, close) and a consistent brand frame, and slides 5 and 12 are well built. But the feature section is mostly small bullet text with generic photos (slides 3, 8, 9), and slide 7 packs two topics into small text next to diagrams with minimal captions. The spec tables on slides 15 to 17 are dense, tiny and hard to read at slide size, and slides 10 and 18 use very small body text, so the deck works better as a leave-behind than as a presented sales deck.

**Worth keeping:** Keep the product-with-callouts layout on slide 5, the numbered icon steps on slide 12, the full-bleed photography on slides 8 and 9, and the consistent title placement and green and dark grey palette.

**Most useful repair:** Enlarge or split the tables on slides 15 to 17, raise the body text size on slides 10 and 18, and fix slide 9 where two features share a page with a photo that does not illustrate them.

**What would change the score:** Replace the bullet-heavy feature slides 7 and 8 with one clear point per slide supported by a diagram or a headline number, add leader lines on slide 5, and cut the near-empty contents and divider pages (2, 13) or make them do more.
