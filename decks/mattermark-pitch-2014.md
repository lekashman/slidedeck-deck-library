But the key diagram on slide 18 is damaged: the colored wedges sit over the earlier slide's labels, so white headings collide with faded text ('Market Sizing', 'Pricing', 'New Product Development' are partly unreadable), and the pale yellow wedge gives weak contrast with white text. **What would change the score:** Simplify slides 11, 13 and 14 to one series or one message each, and cut the divider slides (15, 21, 23) or give them a point.

## Summary

The deck opens with a title and a one-line mission to organize the world's business information, then frames the problem: professionals lack a reliable way to ask business questions and get data-backed answers. It moves through market context and a proposed B2B search product, then sizes the audience from venture capitalists and angel investors to sales and business development professionals and later consultants and bankers. Traction follows, with monthly recurring revenue growth charts, a 2015 revenue goal, and revenue broken down by use case. Later sections cover the core questions customers ask, the data needed to answer them, the number of companies tracked, and a pace target for coverage. The deck closes with the leadership team, with short bios for each founder and key hires, and a final title card. Several slides are worth studying for how they present growth as simple line charts with one headline number and how a single diagram maps data needs to user groups.
