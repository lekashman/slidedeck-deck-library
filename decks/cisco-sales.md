## Review

**7 out of 10, Effective.** Presentation quality, judged with the SlideDeck Co review heuristic (https://slidedeck.co/review-methodology/). The ask, the evidence and the merit of the idea are not rated.

The deck has a strong, consistent identity: a dark navy field, a gradient accent, large light titles, a repeating section tab bar, and a clear route from framing through the marketplace mechanics, products, Splunk and a close. Slide 6 compares MPO and REO in a clean two-column table with readable type, and slide 10 gives its figures a clear size hierarchy. **Worth keeping:** Keep the dark palette with gradient accents, the large light titles, the section tab bar showing the current section, and the MPO and REO comparison layout on slide 6.

**Most useful repair:** Enlarge the body text and shorten the copy on slides 3, 5 and 11, and replace the rotated labels on slide 7 with horizontal lists or a simpler product grid.

**What would change the score:** I would cut the number of text-heavy slides, give slide 10 clearer grouping between the stats and the proof points, and add a plain step diagram for how a partner transacts through the marketplace.

## Summary

The deck is a partner sales presentation from Cisco about its joint go-to-market work with Microsoft. It opens with the title slide and a framing slide that positions Cisco and Microsoft together for cloud, AI, security and hybrid work, then makes a case for why the two companies partner. Next it covers the Cisco advantage, pairing integrated networking, security, observability and collaboration with Splunk analytics, and then explains how partners can sell through the Azure Marketplace using Multiparty Private Offers and Reseller Enabled Offers. A product map follows, grouping offerings by workplace, data center, assurance, security and digital resilience, and a slide shows Cisco Devices for Microsoft Teams Rooms. A Splunk section explains how customers can buy through the marketplace against their Microsoft commitments. The deck closes with customer outcome figures, a partnership summary and a closing call to action. Slides are dense with bullet text and labelled navigation tabs repeat across the footer of each page.
