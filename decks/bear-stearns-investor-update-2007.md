## Summary

The deck opens with a title slide naming the Fixed Income Overview and presenters Jeff Mayer and Tom Marano, followed by a headline-led look at the division's growth. Total fixed income net revenues have roughly doubled since 2002, and a bridge chart attributes that growth to mortgage-backed securities, credit and interest rate businesses. Sections then cover the credit franchise, including credit trading, leveraged finance and distressed debt, and credit derivatives volumes. The interest rate and foreign exchange business follows, then a long mortgage section on origination platforms, servicing, securitization rankings, the mortgage research team, commercial mortgages and CDO/CLO activity. Later slides address sub-prime exposure and hedging tools such as the ABX index, with charts of spread changes and tables of the cost of spread widening. The deck closes with a forward-looking statements disclaimer, after which the title slide is repeated. Slides worth studying include the revenue bridge on slide 3, the credit derivatives volume chart on slide 5, and the origination platform charts on slides 10 to 12, each pairing one headline claim with a clear supporting chart.
