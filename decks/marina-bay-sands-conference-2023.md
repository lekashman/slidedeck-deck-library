## Review

**7 out of 10, Effective.** Presentation quality, judged with the SlideDeck Co review heuristic (https://slidedeck.co/review-methodology/). The ask, the evidence and the merit of the idea are not rated.

The deck has a consistent visual identity (spaced caps titles, teal and gold palette, strong photography) and a clear route from journey to focus areas, goals, venue status, food and circularity, then communication and close. Slide 4 is a well organised grid and slides 5 and 8 pair a clear headline with a supporting image or map. The weaknesses are consequential: the slide 2 timeline and slide 3 labels are very small and low contrast against busy photos, slide 22 shows a tiny unreadable impact statement image, and the communication slides 18 to 20 and waste slides 17 and 21 put titles over busy backgrounds with small captions. The deck reads as a sales brochure, so some of the small text will not work if projected to a room.

**Worth keeping:** Keep the consistent headline, supporting line and image-led panel format of slides 9 to 14, the grid structure of slide 4, and the bold figures in the slide 5 bullets.

**Most useful repair:** Enlarge and simplify the slide 2 timeline, put solid panels behind text on slides 3, 17 and 21, and replace the slide 22 screenshot with a few readable headline figures.

**What would change the score:** I would trim the slide 4 goals list into fewer, larger items and merge the three near-identical Communication slides (18 to 20) and the two plant-based slides (12 and 13) into tighter pages.

## Summary

The deck opens with a title slide reading Sustainable Events at Marina Bay Sands, then sets out the property's sustainability journey from 2011 to 2022 as a timeline. It moves to the key sustainability focus areas (climate response, water stewardship, waste and circularity, sustainable sourcing, biodiversity, and culture and capacity building), followed by the 2023 sustainability goals grouped by category. Later sections cover the carbon neutral venue status, triple platinum certifications, responsible seafood, regional sourcing, sustainable menu options such as plant-based meat, and circularity examples such as upcycled table numbers and menu cubes. A section on event communication shows how planners can display sustainability commitments through speeches, factsheets, menus and physical or digital signage. The deck closes with an impact statement listing partnerships with WWF Singapore, The Food Bank Singapore and Seven Clean Seas, sustainability videos, and a thank you slide. Slides 4, 5 and 6 are good examples of dense data presented in a clear grid, and slides 8 and 9 to 14 show a consistent format of a headline, a supporting line and image-led panels.

## Questions about this deck

Slide 4 lists a 3.5% reduction in potable water per sq. ft. from 2019 and 36 million litres of water through EcoTracker projects as 2023 water stewardship goals.

**What does Marina Bay Sands say about its carbon neutral venue status?**

Slide 5 states that 100% of emissions from MICE's gas and electricity consumption were offset through REC purchase between 2019 and 2022. It also describes a yearly commitment of 25,000 RECs, which translates to 25,000 MWh of clean energy.

The deck has 24 slides. Slide 1 is the title slide, and slide 24 is the thank you slide.

**What certifications does Marina Bay Sands list as triple platinum in its deck?**

Slide 6 lists Platinum ratings from USGBC LEED, the Events Industry Council Sustainable Event Standards, and BCA Green Mark, along with ISO20121 Sustainable Event Management System certification.
