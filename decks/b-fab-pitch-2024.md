## Summary

The deck opens with a title slide introducing circular biomanufacturing based on liquid feedstocks, followed by a framing of the problem: fossil-based chemicals and animal-based products carry heavy CO2 and land-use costs. It then presents b.fab's solution, a proprietary process that turns industrial CO2, biomass waste and sidestreams into formate, methanol, hydrogen and protein, fatty acids and amino acids. Market sections size the opportunity from a global animal feed total down to a European salmon feed protein gap, and describe aquaculture as the beachhead market. Further slides cover the product pipeline, technology and IP, customer traction, the licensing and joint venture business model, a roadmap across technology readiness levels, the team and advisors, and a financial projection with unit economics. The deck closes with the seed ask of 2.5 million euros, its use of funds across go-to-market, team and scale-up, and contact details. Slides worth studying include the market sizing funnel on slide 8, the side-by-side sustainability comparison on slide 10, and the revenue and unit economics slide on slide 14.
