## Review

**8 out of 10, Strong.** Presentation quality, judged with the SlideDeck Co review heuristic (https://slidedeck.co/review-methodology/). The ask, the evidence and the merit of the idea are not rated.

The deck tells one story in a consistent system: dark title and section slides, white one-thought pages, a blue accent word on each, and a fixed footer. It builds slowly with question and answer pacing (slides 5-6, 13-16, 18), and the metaphor photos (duck, tomato, firefighters, swimmers) each carry one word of the claim. The 'WILLING / INVESTED / ...' highlight list (58 onward) gives clear wayfinding through the quote sections. Weaknesses are visible but contained: the quote text overlaps the logo on the last contact-sheet quote slide, some stock imagery is loosely tied to its point, the translucent caption bands sit awkwardly over busy photos, and the pace is long at 103 pages.

**Worth keeping:** Keep the one-idea-per-slide rhythm, the blue keyword accent, the dark section dividers (1, 18, 58) and the highlighted word list that tracks which theme each quote supports.

**Most useful repair:** Fix the overlapping text and logo on the World Series quote slide, and reduce the caption band opacity or reposition it where it covers the photo subject, as on the goldfish and tomato slides.

**What would change the score:** I would trim the long run of metaphor photos and repeated quote pages, and swap the generic stock or mismatched images (M&Ms, mushrooms) for company photos.

## Summary

Nanigans created this internal culture guide to support hiring and fit decisions after growing from 35 to 100 people across four offices in just over a year. It opens with a Deloitte study quote on the value of workplace culture and argues that culture is a core pillar alongside industry, product and customers. A short narrative explains why the company had not discussed its culture before, followed by questions about what defines a great company and its employees. The team identified four themes: fast-paced, analytical, results-driven and collaborative. The middle sections pair staff photos, office life and social events with a repeated grid of descriptive words such as willing, invested and caring. Employee quotations from several departments follow, grouped under headings like Workhard, Playhard and Shared Experiences. The deck closes by restating culture as the basis for hiring, asking the reader whether culture matters to them, and ending with a recruiting call to action. Standout slides include the title slide, the one-line question slides that pace the story, and the photo-and-quote pairings in the middle sections.
