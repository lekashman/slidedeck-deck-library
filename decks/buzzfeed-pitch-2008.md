## Review

**7 out of 10, Effective.** Presentation quality, judged with the SlideDeck Co review heuristic (https://slidedeck.co/review-methodology/). The ask, the evidence and the merit of the idea are not rated.

The deck has a clear route: where we are (2), where we are headed (3), product proof (4-11), revenue model (12-17), competitors (18), vision (19), team (20), contact (21). Product slides 4-11 use large real screenshots with short callout labels and a consistent dark caption bar, so the audience can see the product rather than be told about it. Weaknesses are consequential: the text slides (2, 3, 12, 19) are plain bullet lists with small text in the middle of the canvas and no visual emphasis on the key numbers, several screenshots (4, 9, 10, 15) are too small to read, and the team slide (20) uses tiny type with a large empty area. Slide 18's Venn is clean and readable, but its closing takeaway is smaller than the page needs and the diagram is only a list of names inside two circles.

**Worth keeping:** Keep the full-bleed red title, the consistent dark caption bar on screenshot slides, the annotated screenshots on 10, 13 and 17, and the clear Venn on 18.

**Most useful repair:** Enlarge or crop the screenshots on 4, 9, 10 and 15 to the part that makes the point, and raise the team type on 20 so it is readable at room size.

**What would change the score:** I would turn slide 2 into a few large metric callouts and add a visual of the four revenue streams on 12, instead of plain bullets.

## Summary

The deck opens with a title slide and a 'Where We Are' snapshot listing traffic, a two-editor content model, a patent-pending core technology and monthly burn. It then sets out 'Where We're Headed', covering a one-stop shop for web buzz, traffic growth without hiring editors, a self-serve ad platform and the hires planned. The middle sections walk through the product: a 'Buzz Detected' sample feed, raw algorithmic buzz phrases, the user-generated and aggregated content streams, the web app, optimization metrics and a leaderboard. Next comes the revenue model, with separate slides on advertising as content, distributed promotion through widgets and ads, custom promotion on client landing pages and trend targeting. Competitors are placed on a media-versus-advertising map, followed by a 'How Big Can This Get?' vision slide and a team slide listing founders, advisors and staff with prior affiliations. The deck closes with a contact slide. Slides worth studying include the 'Where We Are' metrics slide, the revenue model overview and the competitor map, which places the company between media and advertising.
