## Review

**5 out of 10, Needs substantial work.** Presentation quality, judged with the SlideDeck Co review heuristic (https://slidedeck.co/review-methodology/). The ask, the evidence and the merit of the idea are not rated.

The core story of monitoring drug levels and adjusting dose is recoverable, and slides 10 to 12 show it in a clear before-and-after sequence with plain captions. But the deck runs mostly as unvarying bullet lists with tiny type on slides 4 and 21, and the route is muddled: management comes at slide 4 before the product is shown, the value proposition appears twice (slides 9 and 16), and the offering and investor slides sit before a technology slide at the end. Several pages have fit and legibility defects: the caption on slide 22 collides with the footer band, slide 23 text overlaps the footer, the chart on slide 13 has overprinted axis labels and unreadable noise, and slide 6 pairs unlabeled drawings with a stock server photo and no explanation.

**Worth keeping:** Keep the three-step chemotherapy chart sequence on slides 10 to 12 with its one-sentence takeaway under each chart, and the cartridge, reader, informatics flow on slide 6 as a simple product map.

**Most useful repair:** Fix the text colliding with the footer band on slides 22 and 23, enlarge or split the dense management and investor lists on slides 4 and 21, and cut the chart on slide 13 down to a readable message.

**What would change the score:** I would reorder to problem, solution, product, evidence, market, team, offering, and turn the bullet-only slides 3, 9, 16, 17 and 18 into fewer, headline-led pages with a clear point each.
