The route is clear and conventional: proposition, team, market, product, value, acquisition, competition, financials, why invest now, exit, then a labelled appendix. Most slides carry one argument under a sentence-style subtitle, and the market chart (4), comparison table (6) and scenario slide (9) are readable and well structured. ## Summary

The deck opens with a title slide and then a one-page proposition covering market size, management, pricing, business model and defensibility. It then runs through the management team, the mobile payments market, the product and its technology, the value offered to users versus traditional and online card processing, and customer acquisition plans. Competition and defensibility come next, followed by a financial model with projections and an investment case with proposed funding rounds. It closes with exit strategies, covering M&A prospects and a potential IPO. An appendix holds financial assumptions, comparable company margins, valuation multiples, responses to investor concerns, partnerships, vision and market analysis. Slides worth studying include the market slide, which pairs a growth chart with opportunity, market change and positioning columns, the value comparison table on slide 6, and the financial scenarios on slide 9. The deck relies on tables, charts and short headline-style titles, and most slides carry one argument.

## Questions about this deck

**What does the Square deck say about the size of the mobile payments market?**

Slide 4 describes mobile payments as a massive, rapidly growing but underpenetrated industry. Its chart, sourced to the Aite Group, shows U.S. mobile payments rising from $16 billion in 2010 to $214 billion in 2015, with a CAGR of 68% for 2010 to 2014.

**What is Square's pricing according to the Square deck?**

Slide 2 lists simple pricing of 2.75% per swipe, with a free iPhone, iPad, Android app and card reader. Slide 6 repeats this, stating a free card reader and a flat 2.75% fee, compared with up to 5% variable plus 30¢ flat rate for traditional card processing firms.

**How many slides does the Square deck have and how is it structured?**

The Square deck has 20 slides. Slide 12 is the Appendix index, and the appendix covers financial assumptions, comparable company margins, valuation perspective, concerns and responses, partnerships, company vision and market analysis.

**What financial projections does the Square deck show?**

Slide 9 projects annual revenue rising from $ 10.59 in 2011E to $ 476.25 in 2015E, with EBITDA of 52.39 in 2015E. Its assumptions include a $20m investment, a 20% stake, a 15% hurdle rate, an IRR of 55%, and a sale or IPO at 2.5x 2015 revenue.
