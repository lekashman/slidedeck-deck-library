## Review

**7 out of 10, Effective.** Presentation quality, judged with the SlideDeck Co review heuristic (https://slidedeck.co/review-methodology/). The ask, the evidence and the merit of the idea are not rated.

The deck has a clear route: transaction terms, then five strategic reasons on slide 4, then slides that follow that order, then financials and process. Slides 7 and 12 each carry one idea with a single chart or number, and the red accent and spaced capitals make a consistent identity. Weaknesses are material, though. Slide 7's headline claims the lowest cost solar plus storage but the chart shows only SolarCity's own cost falling, slides 8 and 9 hold small text and tiny charts, and slides 13 and 14 are bare text with no numbers, visuals or hierarchy beyond red headings.

## Summary

The deck opens with a title slide announcing Tesla's plan to acquire SolarCity, followed by a long forward-looking statements disclaimer and a transaction overview covering pro forma ownership, expected close, approval process and per-share consideration. It then makes the strategic case in a sequence of headline-led slides: accelerating the transition to sustainable energy, creating an integrated sustainable energy company, solar installation cost trends, SolarCity's position as a solar industry leader, Tesla's record of innovation and vertical integration, product development priorities, and cross-selling through Tesla's retail network. Financial slides follow, quantifying $150 million of expected direct cost synergies in the first full year after closing and outlining capital discipline, cash flow and the combined financial profile. The deck closes with a process overview covering independent advisors, fairness opinions, disinterested shareholder approval and the board approval process, then repeats the title slide. Slides 4, 7 and 12 are the most instructive for how a single argument is carried by one headline and one chart or number.

## Questions about this deck

**What cost synergies does the Tesla SolarCity acquisition deck expect?**

Slide 12 states that Tesla expects $150 M of direct cost synergies to be achieved in the first full year after closing. The slide says these synergies are driven by sales and marketing efficiencies and corporate and overhead savings.

**How many slides are in the Tesla SolarCity investor presentation?**

The deck has 15 slides. Slide 1 is the title slide announcing the plan to acquire SolarCity, and slide 15 repeats the title slide.

**What are the consideration terms in Tesla's plan to acquire SolarCity?**

Slide 3 describes an all-stock transaction in which SolarCity shareholders will receive 0.110 shares of Tesla for each share of SolarCity, a value of $25.37 per share based on the 5-day volume weighted average price as of July 29, 2016. The slide shows pro forma ownership of approximately 93.5% Tesla and 6.5% SolarCity, with expected close in Q4 2016.

**What does Tesla's deck say about rooftop solar installation costs?**

Slide 7 shows installation cost per watt falling from $3.16 in 4Q12 to $2.49 in 4Q13, $2.10 in 4Q14, and $1.92 in 4Q15, which the slide labels as the lowest reported quarter. The slide cites SolarCity as the source.
