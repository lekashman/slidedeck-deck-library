## Summary

The deck opens with a title slide naming the four founders and a date in April 2007, followed by a product overview of the Mint personal finance application covering transactions, goals and net worth tracking. It then introduces the team and advisors, sizes the US market and its addressable opportunity, and compares Mint with its key competitor Wesabe and with potential entrants. Further sections cover value to users and to partner companies, user acquisition phases, the business model based on referral fees and future advertising, and a financials section with projected revenue, expenses and earnings. Later slides set out the exit strategy, risks and mitigations, competitive response and the financial assumptions behind the model, with a multiples analysis used to estimate exit value. The deck closes with a Q&A agenda and a risks table. Slides worth studying include the market size funnel on slide 4, the partner comparison table on slide 7, and the risks and mitigation grid on slide 13, which pair each risk with a concrete response in a single view.
