## Review

**7 out of 10, Effective.** Presentation quality, judged with the SlideDeck Co review heuristic (https://slidedeck.co/review-methodology/). The ask, the evidence and the merit of the idea are not rated.

The deck has a clear investor-pitch route (problem, solution, landscape, wedge, proof, team, efficiency, ask), and each slide carries one idea with a plain action title and a takeaway line, so it reads quickly. The charts on slides 5, 8 and 9 are large, well labelled and tied to a one-line conclusion, and the coral header band with the blue section dividers gives a consistent identity. Weaknesses are consequential: slide 1's subtitle is dark text on dark blue and barely legible, slide 13's cohort table is unreadable at this size, slides 2, 3, 12 and 17 use very small text, and slide 18 has no takeaway line to state what the projection means. The product itself is shown only as a tiny screenshot on slide 11, and slides 19 and 20 are sparse.

**Worth keeping:** Keep the action-title plus takeaway-line pattern and the large, clean charts on slides 5, 8 and 9.

**Most useful repair:** Fix the subtitle contrast on slide 1, enlarge and simplify the cohort table on slide 13 with a highlighted key row, and raise the small text on slides 2, 3, 12 and 17.

**What would change the score:** I would give the product a full-size screenshot slide early, since the solution on slide 3 is only described in text, and add a takeaway headline to slide 18.

## Summary

The deck opens with a title slide and a problem statement: business communication still runs on email built for personal use, with a stated 215 billion business emails sent per day. It then presents the solution, a collaborative multichannel email client for teams, followed by a competitive landscape slide placing messaging experience against business readiness. Next come a use case showing shared inboxes as the entry point, a slide of selected customers with quoted feedback, and traction slides covering monthly recurring revenue growth, churn, and a land-and-expand retention chart. The team and capital efficiency sections describe the founders, their backgrounds, and spending to date. The deck then covers acquisition channels, a product roadmap, financial projections for 2016 and 2017, the Series Seed and Series A funding history, and the opportunity in internal communications. It closes with a single thanks slide. Slides worth studying include the traction charts on slides 8 and 9, the shared inbox volume chart on slide 5, and the roadmap grid on slide 17.

Single centred title on a full-bleed patterned field; the title sits at the vertical and horizontal centre of the slide with no footer, no slide number and no other text. Margins are not applicable because the scattered confetti shapes run to the edges.

**Visuals**

Decorative confetti-style pattern scattered across the whole slide: small mint-green quarter-circles, amber/gold quarter-circles and small triangles, coral/orange-red squares and dots, teal squares and dots, pale concentric-ring target icons and small pale striped rectangles (horizontal line bars). Shapes are flat, unoutlined and unshadowed, varying in size from about 0.5% to 2% of slide width, distributed irregularly with the central area around the title kept relatively clear. No photograph, chart or logo.

**Design**

Background: Solid slate blue #3D5A7A with scattered flat confetti shapes. Flat slate-blue background (#3D5A7A) with no gradient. Title is white (#FFFFFF) in a geometric sans-serif bold typeface resembling Futura or Montserrat Bold, all capitals with slight letter-spacing, cap height about 11% of slide height. Confetti accents: mint green #A8DCC0, amber #D99A2B, coral #E8674A, teal #2E8B84, pale blue-grey #9FB8C8 for ring and stripe icons. No borders, no corner radii, no shadows.
