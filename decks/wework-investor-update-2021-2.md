## Review

**6 out of 10, Needs substantial work.** Presentation quality, judged with the SlideDeck Co review heuristic (https://slidedeck.co/review-methodology/). The ask, the evidence and the merit of the idea are not rated.

The core story is recoverable and several slides are well made: the retail analogy on slide 5, the before and after table on slide 11, and the clean KPI tiles on slide 7. But the deck is a filing-style earnings document, and the two full-page disclaimers (slides 2 and 3) sit before any content, in type too small to read. Slide titles are mostly topic labels rather than findings, and slide 14 has ghosted, garbled duplicate axis labels under the waterfall that look like a rendering fault. The closing is legal text with no summary or takeaway, and slide 11 shows memberships falling from 628k to 406k without any framing on the page.

**Worth keeping:** Keep the retail adoption analogy on slide 5, the large-number KPI tiles on slides 7 and 12, and the consistent teal palette and footer system.

**Most useful repair:** Remove the ghost text under the waterfall on slide 14, move the disclaimers to the back, and rewrite topic titles on slides 7, 9, 13 and 14 as the finding each page should leave.

**What would change the score:** I would add a summary slide of the key numbers and next step after slide 17, and cut slide 10's weak logo layout and the dense small text on slides 2, 3, 18 and 19 to an appendix.
