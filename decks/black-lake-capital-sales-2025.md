## Review

**6 out of 10, Needs substantial work.** Presentation quality, judged with the SlideDeck Co review heuristic (https://slidedeck.co/review-methodology/). The ask, the evidence and the merit of the idea are not rated.

The deck has a clear route (who we are, values, philosophy, offer, three case studies, team, contact) and a consistent brand with strong cover imagery, so the core message is recoverable. But the proof is the weakest part: the case study slides (6 to 8) bury outcomes in small, plain paragraphs with no numbers pulled out, and the headline results (10x returns, $50M founder wealth) are set in ordinary body text on slides 2 and 4. Slide 6 shows a pixelated logo, slides 6 to 8 have a decorative photo strip that takes up space while the text above is small, and slides 3 and 5 have small text with little hierarchy. Overall it is competent but needs substantial revision to make its case land.

**Worth keeping:** Keep the full-bleed cover on slide 1, the consistent case study template across slides 6 to 8, the numbered four-point structure on slide 4, and the clear partner versus advisory grouping on slide 9.

**Most useful repair:** Pull the key outcomes (10x cash-on-cash, $50M founder wealth, exit names and dates) into large callouts on slides 2, 4 and 6 to 8, replace the pixelated logo on slide 6, and enlarge the small body text on slides 3, 7 and 8.

**What would change the score:** I would swap the decorative landscape strips for space that carries bigger text or a result figure, and cut the team bios to the few credentials that matter.
