## Review

**6 out of 10, Needs substantial work.** Presentation quality, judged with the SlideDeck Co review heuristic (https://slidedeck.co/review-methodology/). The ask, the evidence and the merit of the idea are not rated.

The route is clear: solution, use cases, origin, how cards work, problems, how Dwolla works, product, network, market, plan. The icon diagrams on slides 5, 9 and 11 are the strongest pages, with consistent icons and orange callouts (FEE, 1-7 DAYS, 3% LOST, <1 SECOND) that make the cost and speed contrast visible. But slides 8, 10 and 12 are unfinished: two near-empty placeholder pages and a 'Secret Sauce' / 'Cool, technical sounding stuff goes here' page, which undercut the deck's credibility and break the flow. Several text slides (6, 7, 13, 14) are dense bullet lists with cramped line spacing, and slide 3 and slide 5 contain very small labels, so the middle of the deck is harder to read than the diagrams.

**Worth keeping:** Keep the dark grey and orange identity with the curved header band, the card-flow diagram on slide 5 with its fee and delay callouts, and the Dwolla flow with the green '<1 SECOND' marker on slide 9.

**Most useful repair:** Remove or finish slides 8, 10 and 12, fix the tight line spacing on slides 6, 7, 13 and 14, and enlarge the tiny labels on slides 3, 5 and 11.

**What would change the score:** I would turn the grouped problem lists on slides 6 and 7 into a few visual claims with one headline number each, and give slide 16 a single clear takeaway instead of a team-count list plus a growth timeline.

## Summary

The deck opens with a title slide and a one-line solution statement: Dwolla lets anything connected to the internet move money quickly, safely and cheaply. It then shows growth through a list of use cases, from individuals and schools to banks and developers, before introducing the founders and the origin of the company in the credit card fee problem. The middle sections build the case in order. A diagram shows how card payments flow and where fees accumulate. A list of problems with existing payment rails is grouped under cost, speed, fraud and bank dependence. A diagram of the Dwolla flow includes a real-time settlement step. Later slides cover the end-to-end product, the technology, the network's advantages, and the market opportunity in the US and globally. The deck closes with a 2013 hiring and expansion plan, a references slide and a closing title.
