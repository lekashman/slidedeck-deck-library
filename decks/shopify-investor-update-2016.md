## Summary

The deck opens with headline metrics of more than 200,000 active merchants and over $1.9 billion in gross merchandise volume in Q3 2015. A safe harbour statement on forward-looking claims and non-GAAP measures follows. It then covers the company's multi-channel commerce platform and its market of entrepreneurs, small and mid-sized businesses, and larger brands. A market-sizing slide sets a global total addressable market against current core geographies. Next comes a company history in the form of a Build-a-Business timeline, along with the partner ecosystem of app developers, theme designers, and agencies. The financial section shows revenue, monthly recurring revenue, and GMV growth with year-on-year comparisons, a gross profit slide, and an operating leverage slide tracking expenses as a share of revenue. Growth vectors and investment highlights come next, and the deck closes with the line 'Make commerce better for everyone.' An appendix reconciles non-GAAP figures to GAAP.
