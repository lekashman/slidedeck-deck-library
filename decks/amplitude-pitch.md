## Review

**6 out of 10, Needs substantial work.** Presentation quality, judged with the SlideDeck Co review heuristic (https://slidedeck.co/review-methodology/). The ask, the evidence and the merit of the idea are not rated.

The deck has a clear spine (measure, retention, analytics as infrastructure, product, tech edge, market, competition, distribution) and a consistent blue identity with sentence-like titles on many slides. Several slides carry a single idea well: the 20X bar on slide 10, the willingness-to-pay funnel on slide 14, and the Facebook example on slide 6. But the execution is uneven: slide 5 is one small sentence in a mostly empty page, slide 8 is a washed-out overlay that hides the content, the cohort screenshots on slides 6 and 11 are unreadable, and the market chart on slide 12 has tiny axis and legend text. The 20X chart has no labels for what the bars compare, and traction (slide 16) is only logos and one line, so the core is recoverable but needs substantial revision.

**Worth keeping:** Keep the funnel on slide 14 that places Amplitude at the top tier by willingness to pay, the large 20X bar on slide 10, and the consistent blue palette with plain sentence titles.

**Most useful repair:** Enlarge or crop the cohort screenshots and the market chart so labels can be read, label both bars on slide 10, and remove or rebuild the faded slide 8 so the claim is legible.

**What would change the score:** Fill the empty slides 4 and 5 with a real visual or merge them, replace the logo-only traction slide with actual numbers, and tidy the crowded logo map on slide 15 and the cramped text boxes on slide 7.

## Summary

The deck opens with a title slide and a team slide introducing the CEO and CTO, both MIT-educated engineers with prior startup and big-tech experience. It then argues the problem: companies must measure user behaviour to improve products, and retention drives growth. Next it positions analytics as core infrastructure, using a Facebook retention study as an example of behavioural cohorting. The solution section describes Amplitude as a powerful analytics product for app teams, covering cohorting, raw data access and pricing at scale. Technical advantages follow, focusing on a claimed 20X data-volume advantage and deeper query capability. Market sizing uses Gartner and Redwood Capital figures on business intelligence and mobile analytics growth. A competitive landscape compares Amplitude with named analytics and mobile marketing tools. Traction, distribution through an enterprise sales process, and long-term advantages close the deck. Slides worth studying include the cohort-definition example on slide 6, the competitive landscape map on slide 14, and the single-claim tech advantage slide on slide 10.
