## Summary

The deck opens with the Brex name and then turns to the team and investors, listing technology and finance leaders alongside backers such as Ribbit Capital, Y Combinator and several payments executives. It then sets the problem: entrepreneurs are often refused credit cards, and corporate cards offer little control. Market slides follow, sizing the card revenue opportunity across global B2B payments, the U.S. Fortune 500 and U.S. venture-backed companies, and explaining why technology companies are the initial focus. Product sections cover better data, better controls, receipt capture and ERP integrations, shown through annotated transaction screens. Later slides cover retention through rewards, referral-only private beta growth, a 'negative churn' customer graduation chart by funding stage, and a unit economics table with reference customers. The deck closes with a short investment value proposition and the Brex name again. Several slides pair a single chart or annotated product screenshot with a headline-style title, which keeps the argument moving from market to product to economics.
