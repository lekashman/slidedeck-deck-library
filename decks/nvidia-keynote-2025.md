## Review

**7 out of 10, Effective.** Presentation quality, judged with the SlideDeck Co review heuristic (https://slidedeck.co/review-methodology/). The ask, the evidence and the merit of the idea are not rated.

The deck has a clear, consistent identity: black ground, green and gold accents, strong stage photography and a repeating quote-plus-visual layout that makes a 21-slide recap easy to follow. Slides 4, 12 and 13 are readable and focused, with the spec callouts on 4 and 12 and the three-curve scaling diagram on 13 each carrying one idea. The main weaknesses are that body text on the contact sheet slides (5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 14, 16, 18, 19) and many labels are tiny and low contrast, and some slides rely on press quotes as headlines instead of stating the point. Slide 12 has a stray duplicate image and an unexplained small product shot, and slides 11 and 17 are image walls with illegible labels, so the route reads as a keynote highlights reel rather than a document that can be read on its own.

**Worth keeping:** Keep the dark brand look, the large stage photography, and the spec-ring layouts on slides 4 and 12 and the clean scaling diagram on slide 13.

**Most useful repair:** Raise body and label text to a legible size and contrast on the text-heavy slides, and clean up slide 12 so the product image appears once and the callouts have a clear focus.

**What would change the score:** I would replace the quote-as-title pattern with short claim headlines, with the press quote as supporting evidence, and add labels or captions that make slides 11, 17 and 18 readable.
